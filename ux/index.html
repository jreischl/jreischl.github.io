The story so far

In the beginning...

Circa late 1990s

As a student at St. Paul's College of Visual Arts, I learned the fundamentals of visual communication, including optical and focal mechanics, spatial relationship, emphasis, contrast, color theory, typography, and illustration.

As a curious DIY'er, I learned the very basics of HTML coding by reverse engineering source code. My first website was an illustration portfolio.

Testing the waters

2000-2005

My initial professional experience was as a design generalist, with one foot in print and the other squarely in digital. I learned marketing-centered design using Adobe's Creative Suite while working as a graphic designer for a publisher and later an advertising agency.

On the side, I picked up numerous graphic and web design projects for bands and record labels.

Crawling forward

2005-2008

I started working as in-house creative for a conglomerate of technology companies and my focus shifted more deeply into digital design as I dedicated more time to web applications, email marketing, and interactive media. At the time, I used Adobe (née Macromedia) Dreamweaver and Flash to design brochureware websites and media.

Higher brain functions

2008-2012

By this time I had transitioned fully to UX design, working almost exclusively on web applications. As smart phones hit the market, the concept of responsive design became imperative. I was working for an airline designing solutions for complex online user flows. This is where I came to understand the concept of usability and user-centered design thinking. I abandoned Dreamweaver's WYSIWYG editing in favor of working directly in HTML, CSS, and Javascript to create semi-functional prototypes which simulated an experience at the highest fidelity.

Human, centered

2012-present

For the past decade, I have worked for a multi-brand, international franchisor. I started as a UX designer, a team-of-one within the Development department, and quickly expanded my purview from web to mobile applications, designing for iOS and Android, both for consumer audiences and enterprise. As our approach to product development matured, I learned the tenets of design thinking and human-centered design. I became a champion of accessibility, even founding a community of practice group on the subject. Though I am still an individual contributor in my role as product designer, I also manage a small team of UX designers.

As design has become more inclusive, iterative, and collaborative, I've introduced DesignOps practices to the organization, establishing comprehensive design systems which tie directly into our workflows.

Oh the places we'll go

The Future

The future is already being written by today's visionary thinkers and the alchemy of creative problem solving that brings those visions to life. As one of those alchemists, rooted in a strong foundation of philosophy, skill, and experience, I look forward to helping deliver it.
